# News Taxonomy — Techcombank CIBG Market Intelligence

News classification taxonomy for the Corporate & Institutional Banking Group (CIBG).

---

## CATEGORY: `macro`

Macroeconomic news affecting the overall economy and the bank's business environment.

### `interest_rate` — Interest Rates
- SBV policy rates (refinancing, rediscount, OMO)
- Deposit and lending rates across the commercial banking system
- Monetary policy related to interest rates (Fed, ECB, SBV)
- **Examples**: "SBV cuts policy rates", "Sacombank raises savings rate to 8.8%", "MB cites 3 factors to lower lending rates"

### `oil_energy_price` — Oil & Energy Prices
- Crude oil prices (Brent, WTI), domestic gasoline prices
- OPEC+ production, global oil demand
- Fuel taxes, fees, strategic petroleum reserves
- **Examples**: "Brent crude drops to $75", "Gasoline price falls VND 5,600/liter", "Environmental tax on fuel reduced"

### `exchange_rate` — Exchange Rates & FX
- USD/VND rates (central, interbank, free market)
- SBV FX policy, market intervention
- Global USD movements (DXY)
- **Examples**: "USD/VND stable around 25,200", "Banks raise USD price to ceiling"

### `gold_commodities` — Gold & Commodities
- Global and domestic gold prices
- Prices of basic commodities (steel, cement, agricultural products...)
- Domestic-global gold price premium
- **Examples**: "Gold drops sharply on strong USD", "Domestic gold premium widens to VND 30 million"

### `gdp_growth` — GDP & Macroeconomic Growth
- Vietnam and regional GDP, economic growth
- Economic stimulus policies, growth targets
- Economic reports/forecasts (ADB, WB, OECD, IMF)
- **Examples**: "ADB: ASEAN GDP down 2.3%", "Double-digit growth target", "OECD warns of US inflation"

### `fdi_trade` — FDI & International Trade
- Foreign direct investment (FDI) into Vietnam
- Imports/exports, trade policies
- International trade relations (US-China trade, EU regulations...)
- **Examples**: "FDI into HCMC up 200%", "EU exports hindered by packaging rules", "Thanh Hoa seeks $24B investment"

### `geopolitical` — Geopolitics
- International conflicts affecting the economy (US-Iran, Middle East, US-China)
- Sanctions, economic embargoes
- Supply chain risks from geopolitical events
- **Examples**: "US strikes Iran", "China launches trade probe on US", "Strait of Hormuz under threat"

### `fiscal_policy` — Fiscal Policy & Taxation
- Public investment, state budget
- Tax policies (VAT, environmental tax, PIT/CIT)
- Government bonds
- **Examples**: "VND 150,000B public investment package for North-South expressway", "VAT on fuel reduced"

---

## CATEGORY: `micro`

Sector-specific news directly affecting CIBG's clients and credit portfolio.

### `real_estate` — Real Estate
- Developers (Novaland, Vingroup, Masterise, Sun Group, FLC, Dat Xanh...)
- Real estate projects (residential, commercial, townships, social housing)
- Property market (prices, transactions, legal, zoning)
- Real estate bonds, developer debt restructuring
- **Examples**: "Novaland reverses VND 3,300B land-use fee provision", "RE lending rates hit 14-16%", "Masterise launches same-day groundbreaking"

### `infrastructure` — Infrastructure & Construction
- Expressways, railways, airports, seaports
- Construction contractors (Coteccons, Hoa Binh, Son Hai, Vinaconex...)
- Construction materials, project costs
- Urban planning, transportation
- **Examples**: "North-South expressway phase 2", "Can Gio mega-port $5B", "Asphalt and sand shortages for construction"

### `banking_finance` — Banking & Finance (Competitors)
- News about other commercial banks (VPBank, MBBank, HDBank, Sacombank, Eximbank, BIDV, VCB, Agribank...)
- Bank business strategies, senior personnel
- Banking M&A, partnerships
- Fintech, consumer finance (Home Credit, FE Credit...)
- **Examples**: "HDBank establishes bank at VIFC", "Former SeABank CEO moves to Sacombank", "Home Credit profits VND 2,000B"

### `financial_services` — Financial Services (CIBG Clients)
- **These are key CIBG clients — positive news about them = business opportunities for TCB**
- Securities companies (VPS, SSI, VNDS, HSC, Mirae Asset, TCBS...)
- Insurance (Bao Viet, Manulife, Prudential, AIA...)
- Investment funds, asset management companies
- Leasing, finance companies (non-banks)
- **Examples**: "VPS raises charter capital to VND 15,000B (VPS is a major TCB client)", "SSI posts record Q1/2026 profit", "Bao Viet issues VND 3,000B bonds"

### `entertainment_travel_hospitality` — Entertainment, Travel & Hospitality
- Airlines (Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet, Bamboo...)
- Tourism, hotels, resorts, hospitality
- Entertainment, events, F&B, lifestyle brands
- Luxury retail (luxury brands, high-end stores)
- Airfares, routes, travel demand, tourism mega-cities
- **Examples**: "Thousands of flights cut", "Audemars Piguet opens store in HCMC", "14,700-ha coastal tourism mega-city", "Vietnam-Qatar flights resume"

### `healthcare` — Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals
- Private hospitals (Vinmec, FV, Hoan My, Thu Cuc...)
- Pharmaceuticals (DHG Pharma, Traphaco, Pymepharco, Imexpharm...)
- Medtech, medical devices
- Health insurance, healthcare policies
- **Examples**: "Vinmec invests VND 5,000B hospital", "DHG Pharma revenue up 20%", "FV Hospital expands new facility"

### `capital_markets` — Capital Markets & Securities
- Stock market (VN-Index, specific stocks)
- Equity and corporate bond issuances
- Investment funds, M&A
- **Examples**: "Stock market up 28 points", "Coteccons issues bonus shares", "MSR transfers to HOSE"

### `diversified` — Diversified Sectors
Catch-all category for sectors not covered by the specialized subcategories above. Covers multiple industries that are part of CIBG's diversified lending and fee-income portfolio.

- **FMCG & Retail**: Masan, Unilever, C.P., Coca-Cola, The Gioi Di Dong, Vincom Retail, domestic consumption trends
- **Energy & Utilities**: EVN, power plants (LNG, renewables), oil refining (Nghi Son, Binh Son), water infrastructure
- **Manufacturing & Industry**: Hoa Phat, Tan A Dai Thanh, Honda VN, industrial parks, manufacturing FDI, supply chains
- **Technology & Telecom**: Viettel, VNPT, FPT, digital transformation, semiconductors
- **Agriculture & Aquaculture**: Agricultural products, aquaculture, food processing, agricultural exports
- **Examples**:
  - "Masan revenue up 12% YoY", "The Gioi Di Dong pays VND 3,000B in dividends"
  - "EVN to invest VND 500,000B in power", "Hiep Phuoc LNG plant VND 19,000B"
  - "Hoa Phat targets VND 210,000B revenue", "Industrial zone investment wave"
  - "Viettel overseas investment up 30%", "Whoever owns chips controls trade"
  - "Agricultural giant partners with German conglomerate", "Durian prices plunge 35%"

---

## CATEGORY: `other`

News not related to macro or micro categories, with minimal direct relevance to CIBG operations.

### `general_news` — General News
- Social news, weather, criminal law unrelated to finance
- International news not affecting Vietnam's economy
- Entertainment, lifestyle news
- **Examples**: "Heatwave in HCMC", "Belarusian President gifts rifle to Kim Jong-un", "House fire on Linh Nam street"

### `fraud_scam` — Financial Fraud & Scams
- Financial fraud, crypto scam platforms
- Financial law violations
- **Examples**: "Golden Fund suspends operations", "Hanagold CEO arrested", "Risk warning: Tikop, Topi"

---

## CLASSIFICATION RULES

1. **A single article may belong to MULTIPLE subcategories** (e.g., "RE lending rates at 14-16%" → `interest_rate` + `real_estate`)
2. **Prioritize the most specific subcategory** — if the article primarily discusses one of the 7 specialized micro sectors (real_estate, infrastructure, banking_finance, financial_services, entertainment_travel_hospitality, healthcare, capital_markets), choose that sector as primary
3. **Macro vs Micro**: If the news affects the entire economy → macro. If it only affects a specific sector → micro
4. **`other`**: Only use when the news is TRULY unrelated to finance/business
5. **`financial_services` vs `banking_finance`**: News about commercial banks (TCB competitors) → `banking_finance`. News about securities firms, insurance, investment funds, leasing (CIBG clients) → `financial_services`
6. **`diversified`**: Use for micro-level news about FMCG, retail, energy/utilities, manufacturing, technology/telecom, or agriculture that does NOT fit into the 7 specialized subcategories above
7. **Output**: Always return `primary_subcategory` (most important) and `secondary_subcategories` (if applicable)
