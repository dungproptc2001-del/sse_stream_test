# Opportunity Identification Methodology

## Purpose
Translate an approved Priority Area into specific, measurable economic opportunities or performance problems.

Opportunity Identification answers:

> **Where exactly should the Bank act?**

It narrows the field before specific resource-consuming initiatives are assessed.

## Trigger
Run:
- when an approved Priority Area requires action;
- when Information Intake or a Significant Event reveals a specific opportunity;
- when Weekly Monitoring identifies a concentrated product / sector / segment deviation;
- on request of MC.

## Opportunity universe
Opportunities may be defined by:
- PRODUCT;
- COMMERCIAL-BANKING SECTOR;
- CUSTOMER / RETAIL SEGMENT;
- CHANNEL;
- GEOGRAPHY;
- PRICING / PROPOSITION;
- PROCESS / OPERATING MODEL;
- OTHER economically distinct area.

## 1. Quantify the opportunity
Where possible identify:
- current PBT contribution;
- plan PBT contribution;
- PBT gap / upside;
- cost-resource requirement;
- ROE / RoRWA;
- size / growth of addressable profit pool;
- leading performance indicators.

## 2. Establish causal thesis
State:
- why the opportunity / problem exists;
- why it is actionable;
- why the Bank is positioned to capture / fix it;
- what is already reflected in the current plan;
- expected horizon.

## 3. Compare alternatives
Compare the opportunity against:
- doing nothing;
- passive continuation of the current operating plan;
- alternative opportunities competing for the same resources.

## 4. Opportunity classification
Classify:
- `HIGH POTENTIAL`
- `POTENTIAL`
- `WATCH`
- `NO ACTIONABLE OPPORTUNITY`

## Principles
- Separate identification of the opportunity from assessment of the proposed solution.
- Quantify the opportunity before debating a project where feasible.
- Avoid choosing an initiative first and then constructing an opportunity narrative around it.
